# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly, useful place for anyone who builds or markets websites, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Give feedback you would be glad to receive: specific, useful, and about the change in front of you.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and say so when you change your mind.
- Credit others' work, and only share what you have the right to share.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualised comments or imagery, threats, doxxing (publishing
someone's private details), sustained disruption, and encouraging any of these.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you represent the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers: use the repository's private reporting form described in `SECURITY.md`
and start the title with "Conduct:" so it reaches the right person. If you cannot use GitHub, the maintainers'
contact route will be listed in `SECURITY.md` once it exists. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will respond, decide on a
fair response (from a private word to a ban) and tell the reporter what happened where they can.

This code adapts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
