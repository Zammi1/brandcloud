# BrandCloud — Logo Guidelines v1.0

October 2026. Applies to the logo only. The app UI lives in a separate project.

## The mark: Modular Cloud (Direction A)
Three library primitives (a quarter-circle, a half-circle and a pill) are set on a 64-unit grid with a constant 4-unit gap. Together they make a cloud. It is a component library and a cloud at once.

- Grid: 64 × 64 units. Live area: x 4–60, y 12–52 (56 × 40).
- Gap: 4 units everywhere. In the wordmark at lockup scale, the stroke is also 4 units.
- **Small-size optical cut** (`brandcloud-mark-small-currentcolor.svg`): the gap widens to 6 units. Use it at 24px and below so the gaps stay at least 1px.

## Wordmark
"BrandCloud". A custom geometric monoline built on a 9-unit stroke, with a cap height of 60 units and an x-height of 40 units. It is pure vector stroke geometry, so no font is needed. Keep the capital B and capital C. Never retype it in a font.
If a production tool needs filled outlines, use "Outline stroke / Expand" on the files and do not change any proportions.

## Colour
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Cobalt (primary surface) | #1256E8 |
| White (logo on cobalt / primary action fill) | #FFFFFF |
| Black (logo on white / action labels) | #0A0A0A |

Approved pairs: white on cobalt (hero), black on white, white on black, and cobalt on white (secondary). No gradients, tints, outlines or shadows.

## Lockups
- Horizontal: the mark is 40 tall, the wordmark is 27 tall (cap height), and the two share a baseline at the base of the pill. The gap is 12 units.
- Stacked: the mark is 56 tall and centred. The wordmark is 205 wide and sits 14 units below the mark.

## Clearspace
X = ¼ of the mark height (10 units when the mark is 40 tall). Keep X clear on every side of the mark and of both lockups.

## Don't
- Rearrange, recolour or rotate the modules, or close the gaps.
- Add gradients, shadows or outlines, or put the logo in a container shape (the favicon tile is the only exception).
- Retype the wordmark or change its letter spacing.
- Place the logo on mid-tone or busy photography without a solid cobalt, white or black field.
