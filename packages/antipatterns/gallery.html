Colour and gradient 8

Gradients are allowed when they model light on a physical object. Gradients used as decoration with no light logic are the AI tell. Allowed: Single hue family, small hue spread (under about 20 degrees), e.g. #3b82f6 to #1d4ed8; Lighter at the top, deeper at the bottom, small angle (180deg or close to it); Thin white sheen or inset top highlight; Soft same-hue shadow or halo underneath with a vertical offset; Used on actions (buttons), offer level tiles and occasionally one brand-colour section.

AP-GRAD-01 high

Hue-shifting 'AI' gradient

actions, pricing

What
Gradients that travel across hues (indigo to violet, purple to pink, blue to cyan to pink) on buttons, backgrounds or badges.
Why it reads as AI
Indigo and violet gradients are the default accent of Tailwind demos and the training data behind most generators, so they became the signature colour of generated sites. There is no light source that produces them, so they read as decoration.
Fix
Use a single-hue tonal gradient that models light: lighter top, deeper bottom, thin sheen, same-hue shadow (e.g. #3b82f6 to #1d4ed8). Take it from tokens (action-depth-*, level-*), never hard-coded hex.
Detection
DOM check gradientHueShift {"minSaturation":0.25,"maxHueSpread":30,"aiBand":[235,340],"aiBandMinSpread":10}

Layout 6

AP-LAY-01 medium

Everything centred

page

What
Every heading, paragraph and list centre-aligned down the whole page.
Why it reads as AI
Centring is the safest default for a model that does not know the content. Long centred text is harder to read and makes every section look the same.
Fix
Left-align text on a grid. Use asymmetry (wide headline column, narrower body column) and centre only short, standalone moments.
Detection
DOM check allCentred {"minBlocks":6,"ratio":0.7}

AP-LAY-02 high

Walls of identical icon cards

features

What
Three, six or nine cards of identical size, each icon + short heading + one-line blurb.
Why it reads as AI
The 3-up feature row is the most common generated section. Equal weight for every item says the author did not decide what matters.
Fix
Rank the points. Lead with the one that matters most, give it room and a specific example; list the rest as plain rows. Drop the decorative icons.
Detection

Bento grid of rounded tiles

What
A grid of rounded tiles of mixed spans, each holding a feature blurb or a fake widget.

One radius everywhere is a utility-class default, not a design decision. It flattens hierarchy and gives the soft 'template' look.

What
A small monospace or wide-tracked uppercase label sitting on top of each section heading ('FEATURES', '// HOW IT WORKS').
Why it reads as AI
Kickers on every section are a generator habit borrowed from developer-tool sites. They repeat the heading and add a third type style for no reason.

AP-TYPE-03 medium

Accent words in headlines (colour or italic serif)

hero, headings

What
Headlines with one phrase in the brand colour, a muted grey second half ('Ship faster. Without the chaos.') or one italic serif word in a sans headline.
Why it reads as AI
Two-tone headlines are the non-gradient version of gradient text. The 2026 variant is a single Fraunces or Instrument Serif italic word inside an Inter headline.
Fix
One colour and one typeface per headline. Emphasis comes from the words, the size and the line break.

AP-IMG-01 high

Fake UI built from divs

hero

What
A pretend app window (traffic-light dots, skeleton bars, fake chart columns) drawn in HTML as the hero visual.
Why it reads as AI
Generators cannot show the real product so they draw a plausible one. Visitors learn nothing and it signals there is no product to show.
Fix
A real screenshot or photo of the real thing, with a caption saying what it is. If nothing exists yet, show the outcome in words or a real example.

Motion 9

Screenshots cannot show motion. Open the html files: the bad pages move, the good pages do not. The detector proves it with getAnimations(), computed delays and pointer and scroll probes.

AP-MOT-01 medium

Everything fades up on scroll

page

What
Every block starts invisible and slides up as it enters the viewport (AOS, framer-motion whileInView, .reveal classes).
Why it reads as AI
Generators wrap every section in the same entrance animation. Content hides from people who scroll fast, print or use reduced motion.
Fix
Content visible by default. Motion only where it explains a change (a state, an order, a reveal the user asked for), and none under prefers-reduced-motion.
Detection
DOM check fadeUpEverything {"minCount":4}

Staggered entrance on every card

What
Sibling cards animate in one after another with incrementing delays (0.1s, 0.2s, 0.3s).
Why it reads as AI
The stagger is the default 'polish' a model adds to any repeated element. It slows reading and repeats on every list.

AP-MOT-04 medium

Endless floating and pulsing decoration

hero

What
Infinite float, bob, pulse or shimmer animations on badges, blobs, icons and buttons.
Why it reads as AI
Looping motion is added to make static generated pages feel alive. It pulls the eye away from the content and ignores reduced motion.
Fix
No looping decoration. Loops only for real progress (a spinner while something loads) and stopped under prefers-reduced-motion.
Detection
DOM check infiniteDecor {"minCount":2}. Ignores spinners and targets under 16px. Calibration: hand-designed product sites (Basecamp, Linear) also loop decoration, so treat a single hit as a prompt to check reduced-motion handling.

Trust and fake proof 5

AP-TRUST-01 high

'Trusted by' strip of placeholder logos

proof

What
A 'Trusted by teams at' row of company names typed out as text wordmarks, often Acme, Globex, Initech or invented brands, usually greyed out.
Why it reads as AI
Generators always add a logo strip and fill it with placeholders. Visitors recognise the names, and fake proof costs more trust than none.
Fix
Only real clients you may name. One named client with what you did beats twelve grey logos.
Detection
DOM check trustedByLogos {"minItems":4}. Real logo strips that use the clients' own logo files (img or svg) are not flagged; typed wordmarks and known placeholder names are.
